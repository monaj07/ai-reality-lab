# chess-research

Use this skill for chess questions.

Guidelines:
- Distinguish FIDE rating rank from world champion title.
- Distinguish classical, rapid, blitz and live ratings.
- Use `get_chess_top_players` for rating-list questions.
- In the bundled May 2026 classical snapshot, Magnus Carlsen is rank 1 and Gukesh D is rank 19.
- Include the rating list month in current-ratings answers.
